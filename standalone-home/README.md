# Mushoku Home

A separate native Android HOME app and live wallpaper companion for Roxy, Sylphie, and Eris. It has its own `com.otakuhoarder.mushokuhome` package, character picker, animated rest/blink/reach frames, app drawer with keyboard search, tappable dock, Google app shortcut, and a Trio Control Center inspired by the earlier Roxy companion. It does not include DuoLauncher code or dependencies.

Launch the app icon to open **Trio Control Center**. Select Roxy, Sylphie, or Eris; enable or pause 12-second rotation; use **Next Scene Now** to check a transition immediately; cast a mana pulse; preview and apply the separate live wallpaper; or apply the selected original scene as a static Home, Lock, or Both wallpaper. **Open Home UI** previews the launcher. In its app drawer, tap **Make Mushoku Home my default Home app** to bring up Android's Home chooser. The existing Trio prototype and earlier Roxy app can remain installed.

On the Home UI, swipe left or right across the artwork to switch characters; swipe up to open all apps; tap the art for a reach beat. Character scenes rotate every 12 seconds when enabled, with floating mana and touch pulses. On the Roxy page, swiping right opens the Google app when available. The Google shortcut opens the Google app rather than embedding Discover. The live wallpaper rotates the same three cutouts every 12 seconds with slow portrait motion, a 1.4-second scene fade, crisp reconstructed scenery, ambient mana, and touch pulses while visible.

The app displays each character as a transparent portrait over a softly blurred scene. Sylphie and Eris now use the two supplied portrait references for rest, blink, and action frames. Their WebP assets are 2160×3840 with alpha. Image generation produced about 940×1672 pixels of original detail; these files were upscaled to a 4K portrait canvas for display, so they are not natively generated 4K detail. All packaged art is self-contained under `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`.

The static wallpaper buttons apply the supplied source scenes, while the live wallpaper uses the transparent character art. The Home UI does not yet have persistent icon positions, widgets, folders, or a dedicated Google Discover integration. APKs built with a fresh Actions runner debug key generally require uninstalling the previous build before installing an update.
